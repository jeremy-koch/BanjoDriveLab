How to read this

  • Swing % is where the "&" falls inside the beat: 50 = straight eighths, 66.7 = triplet swing.
  • Downbeat / offbeat offset moves that instrument's on-beat or "&" notes. Negative = early (pushing), positive = late (laid back).
  • Scatter adds random human timing error (standard deviation in ms).
  • At bluegrass tempos, swinging the band to 60% while the banjo stays straight puts each banjo "&" about 0.1 beat ahead. At 200 BPM that's 30 ms.
  • Research on timing perception suggests differences under ~10 ms are hard to hear. Use the blind test before trusting your ears.
  • In the default rhythm, swing is only audible in the guitar's up-strums (the "&" of 2 and 4). Toggle more offbeat cells to make it more obvious.
